Shape the central event into a hand-built bas-relief tile. Carved contours identify the figures, weapons, and effects; compressed perspective keeps every important element on a single shallow plane. Light rakes across uneven glaze, pooling darker in grooves and breaking on the high points. Choose a restrained celadon, cobalt, or iron glaze appropriate to the scene, with clay visible at a few worn edges. The action must remain readable without polychrome painting. No decorative border, writing, or museum label.
