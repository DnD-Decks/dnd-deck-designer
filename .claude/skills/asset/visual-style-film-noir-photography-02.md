name: Silver Shadows
family: Film Noir Photography
---

Stage the fantasy event for a monochrome motion-picture camera, using life-size costumes, props, and practical effects. A sliver of hard light cuts across the decisive gesture; pools of black conceal secondary detail while preserving the outline of each important subject. Rich silver midtones, fine grain, and restrained haze evoke analog film. Compose the scene as one legible still, not a montage. Avoid miniature figures, glossy CGI, colored light, titles, and filmstrip borders.
