Stage the named action as a small physical diorama and photograph it close up. The characters and props should look hand sculpted and carefully painted, with believable material textures, practical translucent effects, and light falling across real miniature terrain. Choose a camera angle that explains the action immediately. Blur the distant background gently, never the focal weapon, gesture, or impact. Hide the edge of the set; exclude labels and display plinths.
