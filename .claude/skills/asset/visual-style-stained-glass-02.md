---
name: Cathedral Glow
family: Stained Glass
---

Imagine the action illuminated from behind in a medieval stained-glass window. Large ruby, amber, cobalt, and clear panes form the actors, props, and one spare setting; continuous charcoal lead seams hold their outlines together. Give the key contact or magical effect the brightest transmitted light. Keep anatomy and scenery simplified enough that the story reads at card size. Omit lettering, saints' halos unless the scene calls for one, and decorative framing.
