---
name: Arcade Afterglow
family: Pixel Art
---

An imagined fantasy game pauses on its most telling frame. Every contour is chosen pixel by pixel: a decisive silhouette, a few sharply separated planes of depth, and sparse animated-looking sparks frozen at the point of impact. Work within a small coherent palette, letting carefully placed color clusters and restrained dithering suggest light without abandoning the chunky square grid. The named action should survive both enlargement and reduction. Do not add scanlines, a display device, menus, health bars, typography, or a frame.
