---
name: Lantern Stage
family: Paper Shadow Theater
---

Inside a tiny lantern stage, precisely cut figures hover at different distances from a glowing vellum screen. Their shadows swell and soften across the paper, while the foreground cutouts remain tactile and sharp. A raised arm, blade, wing, or magical shape is held at the moment when its relation to another form tells the whole story. Use restrained ink-black, parchment, and a single surprising colored sheet. Let pins, fibers, and slight irregularities hint at handcraft without obscuring the event. No lettering, painted faces, frame, or theatrical curtains.
