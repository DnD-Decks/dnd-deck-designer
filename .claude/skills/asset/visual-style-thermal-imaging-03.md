name: Living Temperature
family: Thermal Imaging
---

The scene seems glimpsed by an uncanny instrument that sees only warmth. Cold architecture sinks into indigo-black; living bodies smolder in layered crimson and magenta; a touch, strike, or burst of magic flashes in molten gold and white. Heat diffuses softly beyond edges, yet the essential contours remain exact enough to identify the subject and its action without ordinary lighting. Treat magic as a coherent thermal phenomenon, not arbitrary rainbow smoke. No digital interface, timestamps, measurement scales, text, or frame.
