Treat the entire image as a piece of textile art, not a painting of people wearing embroidered clothes. Thread direction shapes the figures; satin stitches catch the light, rougher stitches describe ground and shadow, and a little metallic thread marks the focal action. Keep the palette limited and the silhouettes clear despite the tactile detail. Let repeated stitch rhythms unify the surroundings without crowding the subject. Omit captions, heraldry, and woven borders.
