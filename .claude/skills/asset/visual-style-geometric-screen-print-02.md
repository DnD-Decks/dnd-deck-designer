---
name: Interlocking Planes
family: Geometric Screen Print
---

Interpret the scene as a limited-run screen print. Simplify people, weapons, and surroundings into large interlocking color planes with hard edges and minimal interior detail. Let the main action interrupt the composition with a clear diagonal or contrasting shape. Use three to five coordinated inks, slight paper grain, and subtle registration imperfections. Avoid realistic light, soft blending, tiny marks, lettering, and framing.
