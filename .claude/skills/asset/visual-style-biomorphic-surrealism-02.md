---
name: Dancing Lines
family: Biomorphic Surrealism
---

Reduce the scene to a few lively organic forms floating on a warm pale ground. Draw with economical, calligraphic black lines and assign each strong color a purpose: a red focal object, a blue counterform, a yellow spark. Distort scale and anatomy freely, yet retain enough recognizable structure to tell what is happening. Let dots, arcs, and stars carry energy without becoming game icons. Keep abundant breathing room and exclude text and framing.
