---
name: Layers of Ink
family: Geometric Screen Print
---

Think in layers of ink rather than paint: one broad shape for the environment, another for the moving figure, an arresting countershape for its target, and a final accent at the point that matters. Each layer should have a distinct color and a confident edge. Paper texture and minute misregistration give the picture a printed life, but never weaken the silhouette. The result should feel graphic, rhythmic, and immediate from across the table. No gradients, intricate textures, captions, or decorative border.
