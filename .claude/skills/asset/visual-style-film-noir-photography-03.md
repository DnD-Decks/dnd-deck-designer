---
name: Midnight Negative
family: Film Noir Photography
---

An impossible fantasy action is captured as though it happened before a 1940s cinematographer's camera. Human-scale performers and tangible props occupy a real set; rain, smoke, and practical light reveal only the essential movement. A blade or spell catches a blade-thin stripe of white as the rest of the frame falls through silver gray into velvet black. Hold the instant at which the action becomes unmistakable, with focus on the subject and deep space behind it. Preserve the grain and imperfections of a photographic negative. No dollhouse scale, illustration, modern digital glow, words, or frame.
