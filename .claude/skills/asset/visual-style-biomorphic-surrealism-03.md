---
name: Whimsical Scene
family: Biomorphic Surrealism
---

Let the named action unfold as a whimsical constellation of creatures, objects, and gestures. A sword might be one unmistakable sweeping form; the evading body a bent, startled shape; a tiny burst of red and yellow the moment they touch. Ink-black lines dance between islands of clear blue, warm red, and sunlit yellow on unpainted ivory. Be surprising in proportion and placement, but never lose the literal event beneath the play. Aim for childlike economy with deliberate composition, not cubism or complete abstraction. No words, labels, or enclosing border.
