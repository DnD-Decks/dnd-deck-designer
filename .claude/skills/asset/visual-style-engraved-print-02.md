---
name: Cut Lines
family: Engraved Print
---

Render the scene as a woodcut and copperplate engraving. Sharp incised contours establish the figures and objects; varied hatching carries their volume, movement, and shadows. Leave broad quiet areas of paper around the focal action so the image does not become a thicket of lines. Use black ink, aged ivory, and a single muted accent. The subject must read before its fine marks do. Omit captions, signatures, and framing.
