An embroidered fantasy scene on woven cloth. Build recognizable figures and objects from visible stitches, layered thread, and small appliqué shapes. Use deep textile dyes and a restrained metallic accent. Clear silhouettes and gesture should explain the action at card size. No lettering, heraldic labels, or ornamental frame.
