---
name: Sixteen-Color Quest
family: Pixel Art
---

Design a game scene with disciplined 16-color pixel art. Large pixel clusters establish the figures and props; selective dithering gives atmosphere and volume without blurring the grid. Spend the brightest few colors on the precise contact, effect, or object named in the scene. Compose for instant recognition at card size, with clear negative space around the action. Pixels must remain uniform squares; avoid mixed-resolution details, HUD elements, lettering, and decorative frames.
