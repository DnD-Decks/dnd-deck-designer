---
name: Shards of Radiance
family: Stained Glass
---

The scene is made of luminous glass fragments held in an intricate but disciplined web of lead. A blade, hand, creature, or spell remains unmistakable through its silhouette, while a few radiant panes mark the instant of motion or impact. Backlight sends jewel colors across the composition; darker glass gives the central event room to shine. Let the material feel brittle, imperfect, and radiant, with slight bubbles and uneven pigment inside each pane. Avoid a church setting unless the scene names one, and add no words, border, or emblems.
