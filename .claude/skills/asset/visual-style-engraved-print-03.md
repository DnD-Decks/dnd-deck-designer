Imagine a fantasy scene pulled from the plates of an old master printer: a blade's arc cut into paper, a foe emerging from a black mass of parallel strokes, light recovered from untouched ivory. Let crosshatching tighten around the decisive gesture and dissolve into open paper elsewhere. A small flash of one accent color may mark the moment of impact. Make the print tactile, severe, and legible when reduced. Avoid photographic shading, full-color painting, text, and ornate margins.
