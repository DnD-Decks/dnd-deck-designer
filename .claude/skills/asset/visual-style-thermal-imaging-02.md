---
name: False-Color Field
family: Thermal Imaging
---

Translate the entire event into a false-color thermogram. Bodies and objects retain their distinct silhouettes, but their surfaces reveal gradients of heat: midnight blue and purple recede, crimson and orange approach, and the point of contact burns white. Allow a little sensor grain and blooming at the hottest edge, while preserving the named weapon, creature, or spell as a readable form. Keep the field uncluttered and remove every instrument overlay or written legend.
