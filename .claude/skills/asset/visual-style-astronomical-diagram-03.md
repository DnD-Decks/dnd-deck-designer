---
name: Midnight Observatory
family: Astronomical Diagram
---

On a field the color of a midnight observatory, a handful of luminous paths trace an impossible but intelligible event. The central figures remain concrete: their outlines tell us who acts and what is touched, while arcs, tangent lines, and a brief starburst reveal the near miss or collision. Give the geometry the precision of an astronomer's instrument and the wonder of a sky map. Let darkness and generous spacing make each mark count. Ivory light and one spectral accent are sufficient; avoid dense occult ornament, equations, labels, and enclosing rules.
