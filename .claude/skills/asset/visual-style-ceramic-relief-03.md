---
name: Kiln-Fired Legend
family: Ceramic Relief
---

The moment has been pressed into wet clay and survived the kiln. A bold raised silhouette emerges from a low, quiet field; incised lines supply only the gestures and contact points needed to understand the scene. Gloss gathers along a lifted blade or spell, while matte recesses keep the background still. Slight cracks, glaze runs, and fingerprints reveal the object as fired earth rather than a digital sculpture. Let one strong glaze and directional raking light do the work of color and depth. Exclude an ornamental plaque edge, inscriptions, and painted illustrations.
