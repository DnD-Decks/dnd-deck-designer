---
name: Layers of Paper
family: Paper Shadow Theater
---

Construct a small theatrical scene from several layers of cut cardstock. Foreground actors and objects are crisp profiles; receding scenery is lighter and simpler. A hidden lamp casts soft, believable shadows between the layers, revealing their physical depth. The decisive gesture must read from the silhouette alone, with one colored paper shape reserved for the impact or spell. Preserve visible cut edges and paper texture; avoid painted detail, captions, and an enclosing proscenium.
