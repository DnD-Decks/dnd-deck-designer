---
name: Story Cloth
family: Embroidered Tapestry
---

A story sewn into cloth: dark dyed ground, figures pieced from colored fabric, gestures drawn in long lines of thread. Dense stitches gather at the critical contact, while quieter, looser stitching lets the rest recede. Flecks of tarnished gold or silver catch the thread at the image's heart. Preserve the slight irregularity of human craft, visible weave, and raised seams, yet make the action legible from across a table. It should feel like a dramatic tapestry fragment rather than a flat digital texture. No written motto, heraldic shield, or decorative perimeter.
